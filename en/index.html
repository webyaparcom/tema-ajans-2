Working principles

Five rules on our desk

Drag a card or flip with the arrows; every project starts in this order.

  1. 01edit_note

    Rule 01

    Sentence first

    Before drawing a logo, we write the brand’s one-line promise together.

    swipe
  2. 02hearing

    Rule 02

    Listen to the audience

    We start with short interviews and field notes instead of desk assumptions.

    swipe
  3. 03straighten

    Rule 03

    The metric is written first

    Every campaign has a metric written up front; the report follows it.

    swipe
  4. 04verified_user

    Rule 04

    No work without permission

    The work and client names shown are samples made for fictional brands; a real client’s work is never published without written permission.

    swipe
  5. 05signpost

    Rule 05

    Permits before outdoor

    For billboards and posters, municipal advertising permits are followed up with the client.

    swipe

Timeline

Eight weeks from brief to report

Milestones of a typical brand refresh project.

  1. Week 1

    Brief and listening

    Goals, audience and budget are gathered on one page.

  2. Week 2

    Brand sentence

    Three alternative sentences are presented and one is chosen.

  3. Weeks 3–5

    Identity and campaign

    Visual language, the first campaign and a content calendar are produced.

  4. Week 6

    Launch

    The campaign goes live; outdoor work only after permits.

  5. Week 8

    Measurement report

    A plain results report against the original goal.
